Why Extreme Weather Is Becoming More Common

By GreenFuture

Climate change is one of the defining challenges of our time

Understanding the science is the first step toward meaningful action. Human activities have increased the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, changing Earth's energy balance and contributing to long-term warming.

The good news is that solutions already exist. Cleaner energy, efficient buildings, sustainable transportation, ecosystem restoration, responsible consumption, and collective action can all contribute to a healthier future.
